Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Call or message and we’ll happily help.

What does a styling consultation include?

A relaxed, practical conversation about your space, how you live and your budget. You’ll come away with tailored ideas on layout, colour, furniture placement and décor. The exact inclusions are confirmed when you enquire, so they suit your project.

Do I need to buy new furniture?

Not necessarily. Often the biggest improvement comes from rearranging and restyling what you already have. If something is genuinely missing, we’ll suggest a small number of well-chosen pieces rather than a full refit.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Yes — it’s the heart of what we do. Furniture & décor styling is about making the most of what you already own, then layering in the finishing touches.

Do you work with rental properties?

Yes. Styling relies on placement, colour, textiles, art and lighting, so it can transform a rental without permanent changes. If an idea involves your walls or fixtures, check your lease first.

Do you style Airbnb properties?

Yes. Short-stay styling makes Airbnb and other short-stay properties more inviting for guests and more photo-ready for your listing.

Do you provide décor sourcing?

Yes. We can source cushions, artwork, lighting, rugs and accessories chosen to suit your style, your space and your budget.

What areas do you service?

Sydney & Surrounds. If you’re not sure whether you’re within range, just ask.

How much does styling cost?

It depends on the size and scope of your project — a single-room refresh is very different to a whole-home restyle. Get in touch with a few details and you’ll receive advice and a quote tailored to your space and budget.
